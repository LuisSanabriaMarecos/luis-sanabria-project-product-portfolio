# Luis Sanabria — Project & Product Portfolio

A recruiter- and client-facing portfolio with three focused views:

- [Project Management](project-management/)
- [Product Delivery](product-management/)
- [Freelance Project & Product Support](freelance/)

The professional experience shown here comes from a supplied CV. Any practice artifact is explicitly labeled **Illustrative work sample** and is not presented as client work.

## Positioning

- **Project Management:** digital delivery, cross-functional coordination, Agile practice and technical operations.
- **Product Delivery:** a transition-ready view grounded in Scrum leadership, product initiatives, customer feedback and market research.
- **Freelance Support:** service offers based on employment experience, without claiming invented freelance engagements.

## Content accuracy

- Street address and phone number are intentionally excluded.
- Conflicting or insufficiently contextualized percentages from the source CV are intentionally omitted.
- “Product Manager” is not presented as a past formal job title; the product view is positioned around adjacent delivery evidence.
- Tools listed in the CV are not assigned to a specific initiative without supporting context.
